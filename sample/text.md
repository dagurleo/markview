## Line breaks

Two trailing spaces  
break the line, so does a backslash\
and so does `<br>`.<br>Next line.

A paragraph that keeps going for a while to show how a long line wraps at the
width of the column, which is the width most comfortable for body text, and
keeps going a little longer so the wrap is easy to see.

## Alerts

> [!NOTE]
> Useful information that users should know.

> [!TIP]
> Helpful advice for doing things better.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Key information users need to achieve their goal.

> [!WARNING]
> Urgent information that needs immediate attention.

> [!CAUTION]
> Advises about risks or negative outcomes.
